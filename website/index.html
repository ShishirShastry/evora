Why Evora

Everything a pro design tool should do. None of the strings.

Most design tools charge for every editor, keep your work in their cloud and hold their best features back for paid plans. Evora doesn't.

Free for everyone, for good

Every feature, for every person, on every file. No plans, no per-editor seats and no “upgrade to unlock”. Bring your whole team, your clients and your developers.

Your files stay yours

Evora is local-first. Designs are saved on your own computer as open .evora files and keep working with no internet connection. Your designs don't leave your computer unless you choose to share them.

Real-time, together

Edit the same file at once with live cursors and selections, follow a teammate around the canvas, and leave comments with @mentions. Changes made offline merge when you reconnect.

Host it yourself

Run Evora's team server on your own machine or cloud. Your designs, your server, your rules, with guides for Docker, HTTPS behind nginx or Caddy, and backups.

Open source, no lock-in

MIT licensed and built in the open. Read the code, fork it and extend it with plugins. No company can take your tools away or change the price on you.

Bring your work with you

Open Figma .fig files and SVGs as editable layers, and export PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF. Switching to Evora doesn't mean starting over.

Features

One tool, from first idea to final handoff.

Design, prototype, build a design system and hand off to developers, all in the same file.

Design

Design anything, precisely

An infinite canvas with frames, shapes, pen and pencil, rich text and images, plus smart guides, snapping and measurements that keep everything pixel-perfect.

  • Vector networks and boolean shapes that stay editable
  • Auto layout with wrapping, padding, gaps, hug and fill sizing
  • Gradients, image fills, shadows, blurs and blend modes
Prototype

Prototypes that feel real

Link screens together and present them on phone, tablet or desktop frames. Test the flow before a single line of code is written.

  • Clicks, hovers, key presses and timers
  • Smart animate, overlays and scrolling
  • Variables and conditions for real logic
Handoff

Developer handoff, included

Developer Mode is part of Evora for everyone, not an extra seat. Developers inspect any layer and copy what they need.

  • Sizes, spacing, colours and type, measured
  • Code for CSS, SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose
  • Export PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF at any scale
Design systems

A design system that scales

Build once, reuse everywhere. Keep products consistent across files and teams with components, styles and variables.

  • Components and variants with overrides and properties
  • Colour, text and effect styles
  • Variables with modes for light and dark themes
  • Team libraries everyone can use, with updates you accept in one click

Version history

Automatic and named versions. Compare side by side or overlaid, and restore in one click.

The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so your computer may ask you to confirm the first time you open Evora. Every installer is built in public by GitHub Actions from the open-source code.

  • Windows: if you see “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open the .dmg and drag Evora into Applications. If macOS says it can’t verify Evora, choose Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x Evora-*.AppImage) and open it, or install the .deb with sudo apt install ./evora_*_amd64.deb.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Evora really free?

Yes. Every feature is available to everyone, with no paid plans, no seat limits and nothing locked away. Evora is open source under the MIT licence, so you can use it for personal and commercial work alike.

Can AI agents design in Evora?

Yes. Evora's MCP server lets Claude Code, Cursor, Claude Desktop, VS Code and other AI agents create and edit Evora designs: screens with auto layout, shapes, text, colours and shadows. Download evora-mcp.mjs from the latest release, connect it to your agent, and open what it makes in Evora to refine it. Everything stays on your computer. How to set it up.

Do I need an account?

No. The desktop app saves your files on your computer and works without an internet connection. Accounts only come into it when you want to work with others through a team server: one you host yourself, or one your company runs.

Can I open my Figma files?

Yes. Open a .fig file and its layers, components, styles, variables, auto layout and prototype interactions come in as editable Evora layers. It was checked against a real Figma file with more than 7,600 layers, and every layer came through. SVG files open as editable layers too. If something in your file doesn't come through as expected, please tell us on GitHub.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

Evora's installers aren't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS don't recognise who made them. On Windows, SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, the first time you open Evora macOS says it can’t verify the app: choose Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway next to the message about Evora. You only need to do this once. The installers are built by GitHub Actions straight from the public source code, and you can check how in the repository.

How does working together work?

Start Evora's collaboration server (or use one your team already runs), invite people to a workspace or share a file by link, and edit together live with cursors, selections, following and comments. If someone goes offline, their changes merge when they reconnect.

Which download do I need for my Mac or Linux PC?

On a Mac, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it lists a Chip such as Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, get the Apple silicon version; if it lists an Intel Processor, get the Intel version. On Linux, the AppImage runs on almost any 64-bit distribution and updates itself; the .deb installs Evora into the menus of Debian, Ubuntu and their relatives, and is updated by installing the newer .deb.

Can I contribute or build plugins?

Please do. Bug reports, ideas and code are welcome on GitHub, and the plugin guide shows how to build sandboxed plugins and widgets, with four examples to start from.
